# health problems associated with computers
- aching hands, shoulders, back or head
- RSI
	- repatitive strain injury
- slow and tired eyes

# prevention
1. chair that supports your lower back and is adjustable (e)
2. feets should rest on the floor or  a footrest (f)
3. keyboard at same height like your elbows & arms parallel to the work surface (c)
4. monitor at or just below eye level (b)
5. arms length distance to monitor, about 50-70cm (a)
6. monitor stand that lets the monitor turn around or up and down (d)

# Letter to my boss

Dear Mr. X,

I was asked by the company to give you advices for better handling with the employee ergonomics. So I did some questioning and worked out two points but first of all I want to tell you that we already have a good handling over all. We have adjustable and supportive chairs. Tables that have many space. Good monitors who are adjustable to eye level. And very comfortable headsets.
My first point on the list is a simple and cost-effective thing, wrist rests. After long time typing the employees notice pains in the wrists because of the hard edges of the tables.
Secondly I want to notice that we have just two options. Either have the blinds open and get blinded by the sun or have the blindes closed and turn on the lights. As a solution we would love to get plissees. Then we can adjust them where the sun is and dont need to lower the blindes completely.
Thanks for you attention.

Sincerely Tim
