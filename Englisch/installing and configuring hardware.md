## Tips about electricity
• Never work on the inside of your PC with the power turned on. Before
you start working, ensure that the power supply is disconnected from
the mains. Always make sure that all network cables are disconnected.
• You should only switch on the power to your PC when the housing is
on.
• You should never try to __dismantle__ the PSU as it contains very dangerous
mains voltages, even when not connected to the power supply.
## Tips about static
• Always wear an antistatic wrist band when __installing__ parts to your PC.
• __ensure__ against damage to sensitive components by not __removing__ them from their
antistatic bags until you are ready to install them.
• When installing PC parts, __avoid__ carpeted areas, where there is often a lot
of static. Don't wear synthetic clothing as this also creates static.
